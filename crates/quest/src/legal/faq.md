# **FAQ**

## **_General_**

### _When did the game start?_

Orientation Quest gameplay began on Sunday, August 16, 2026 at 6:00 PM EST.

### _When does the game end?_

Orientation Quest gameplay ends on Sunday, August 23, 2026 at 6:00 PM EST. After this point, no more Challenges may be completed, and ScottyCoins can no longer be earned.

### _I selected the wrong housing community! What should I do?_

Please let us know through the "Report Issues" button on the Information Page, or through [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1A9kclLbhBInQLIEo8DO7f5fsX_MiAVX-Fa2v6Nsa15U/edit?edit_requested=true#settings), and we will resolve this.

## **_Challenges_**

### _When can I start earning Gemstones?_

You can start earning Gemstones on Monday, August 17, 2026 at 12:00 PM EST.

### _When does Gemstone collecting end?_

Gemstone collecting ends on Friday, August 21, 2026 at 8:00 PM EST. After this point, Carnegie Cup Progress is locked, and no more Gemstones can be earned. You may still complete Challenges until gameplay ends, however.

### _If my housing community needs to be switched in the app, will my Gemstones be reset?_

No, your Gemstones will be maintained if you need to switch your selected housing community. Please use the "Report Issues" button on the Information Page to resolve an issue with an incorrect housing community.

## **_Terrier Trade_**

### _Can I still purchase items from the Terrier Trade after gameplay ends?_

Yes, you may continue to purchase items from the Terrier Trade after gameplay ends, up until Friday, September 25, 2026 11:59pm EST. After this point, the in-app Terrier Trade will close, and items can no longer be bought or refunded.

### _Where and when can I redeem my prizes?_

You can start redeeming your prizes on Tuesday, August 25, 2026 at the Community Life Office (during normal business hours). The last day to redeem your prizes is Friday, October 9, 2026 11:59 PM EST.
